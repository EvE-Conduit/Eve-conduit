# Discord — User guide

The Discord plugin connects your account on this site to your Discord account and puts you on the corporation's
Discord server in one click. After that, your Discord roles and nickname follow your groups, your state and your main
character on this site, so you don't need to ask anyone for roles.

## Where to find it

- **Discord** in the main menu opens your Discord page (`/p/discord`).
- The **Discord** dashboard widget shows a **Link** button if you haven't linked yet, or your Discord name, roles and
  status once you have.

If the page says "Discord isn't set up yet", your leadership hasn't connected a Discord server to the site.

## Linking Discord and joining the server

1. Open **Discord** from the menu.
2. Check the **You'll get** box. It shows the nickname you'll have on the server and the roles that come with your
   groups.
3. Click **Link Discord and join**.
4. Sign in to Discord if asked, and allow access. The site asks only for your identity (`identify`) and for
   permission to add you to the server (`guilds.join`). It never sees your Discord password or messages.
5. Discord sends you back to the site. You see "Linking your Discord account and adding you to the server…" and then
   your Discord page with a **Linked** badge.

You are now on the server with your roles. Click **Open Discord** to go straight to it.

If you see "You don't have access to the Discord server" instead of the link button, your state or groups don't
include Discord access. Ask your leadership.

## Your Discord page after linking

The page shows:

- Your Discord name and the nickname you have on the server.
- When you linked and when your roles were last checked.
- **Your roles**: the roles this site gave you. Roles someone gave you by hand on Discord are not listed and are never
  touched.

Buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open Discord** | Opens the server in Discord. |
| **Fix my roles** | Checks your roles and nickname right now. You can press it once every 30 seconds. |
| **Unlink** | Takes away the roles this site gave you and forgets the link. You can link again at any time. |

## How your roles and nickname stay up to date

- When you join or leave a group, your state changes or you pick another main character, your roles change
  straight away.
- Everyone is also checked every 6 hours.
- Your nickname is set from a format your leadership chooses, for example `[TCORP] Pilot One`. If the format is
  empty, you can pick your own nickname on Discord. Nicknames are cut to Discord's 32-character limit.

## Temporary access links

If you have the permission, **Temporary access** in the menu (or the **Temporary access** button on the Discord
page) lets you bring someone without an account here onto the server for a while.

1. Press **New link**.
2. Say what it's for (your guests see this), how long it lasts, and optionally how many people may use it.
3. Press **Make link**, copy it and send it to your guests.

What your guest does:

1. They open the link and press **Join with Discord**.
2. Discord asks them to confirm.
3. They are on the server with the guest role, and the page tells them when their access ends.

When the link runs out, everyone who came in through it is removed from the server, within a couple of minutes.

On the **Temporary access** page each link shows how long it has left, how many times it was used and who came in
through it.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy link** | Copies the link again |
| **Withdraw** | The link stops working and its guests are removed from the server now |
| **Remove** (next to a guest) | Removes that one guest; the link keeps working for others |

Good to know:

- Someone who is already on the server can't use a link. They are told so, and nothing changes for them.
- If you lose the permission to hand out access, your links stop working and their guests are removed.
- A guest who later becomes a member and links their Discord account here stays on the server.

## Notifications

You get a notification (category **Discord**) titled "Your Discord account was unlinked" when a manager unlinks
your account or removes you from the server. You can mute this category under **Settings → Notifications**. You
don't get a notification when you unlink it yourself.

## Compliance

Your site may require you to be on the Discord server. If so, until you have linked Discord, or while you are not on
the server, your account counts as not compliant and you are told so in your compliance notification. Link Discord,
or rejoin the server, to fix it. Leaving the server is noticed at the next check, at most 6 hours later.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**"Your Discord roles couldn't be updated" with "Not on the server".**
You left the server or were removed. Click **Join the server** in the warning to come back.

**I'm missing a role I should have.**
Click **Fix my roles**. If the role still doesn't appear, the group you are in may not have a Discord role mapped, or
the bot can't give that role. Tell your leadership; the warning on your page says what went wrong.

**"Your roles were just checked; try again in 30 seconds".**
Wait 30 seconds before pressing **Fix my roles** again.

**"That Discord account is already linked to another member".**
Each Discord account can be linked to one member only. Unlink it on the other account first, or ask a manager.

**"This sign-in with Discord has expired or came from another browser".**
Start again from the Discord page in the same browser.

**"You cancelled the sign-in with Discord" or "Discord wasn't linked".**
Click **Try again**.

**Unlinking says the account stays linked.**
The site couldn't reach Discord to take your roles away, so it kept the link to try again. Wait a minute and click
**Unlink** again.

**My nickname changed back.**
Your nickname is managed by the site when your leadership has set a nickname format. Changes you make on Discord are
put back at the next check.

**I want to use a different Discord account.**
Click **Unlink**, then click **Link Discord and join** and sign in with the other Discord account.
