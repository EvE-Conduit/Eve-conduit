# Skill Plans — User guide

Skill Plans tells you what to train and in what order. Leadership shares plans with everyone, and you can make your
own. For every plan you see how far each of your characters is, how long the rest takes, and you can copy the plan (or
only what one character still needs) straight into the game.

## Where to find it

- **Skill Plans** in the sidebar opens the overview.
- The **Skill plans** dashboard widget shows the shared plan you are closest to finishing, with time left and how many
  shared plans you have done (for example `3/7 done`). Click it to open the plan.
- Press Ctrl+K and type part of a plan name to jump to it. Results show as "Shared plan" or "My plan".

## The overview

The overview has two tabs:

- **Shared plans**: plans leadership made for everyone.
- **My plans**: your personal plans. Nobody else sees them.

Each row shows the plan, its category, the number of skills, **My best character** (the character closest to
finishing it), progress and **Time left**. Use the search box ("Plan or category") to filter the list.

## Reading a plan

Open a plan to see:

1. **Character cards**: one per character on your account, with percent done and time to go. "Skills not synced yet"
   means the character sheet has not synced that character's skills. Click a card to pick that character.
2. **Stat cards** for the picked character: percent done, **Time left** and **Skill points left**. If part of the
   time is already in the skill queue, the hint says how much.
3. **Skills**: every skill level in training order, prerequisites first, with skill points. For the picked character
   each level is marked **Trained**, **In queue** or **To train**, with its training time.

Training times use the attributes ESI reports now, implants included. They do not plan for remaps or boosters.

## Copying a plan into EVE

1. Open the plan.
2. Do one of the following:
   - Click **Copy for EVE** to copy the whole plan.
   - Pick a character, then click **Copy what <character> needs** above the skill list to copy only the levels that
     character has not trained yet.
3. In the game, open **Skill Plans** (or the skill queue) and import from the clipboard.

The copied text has one line per level, for example `Gunnery 4`. If the character has nothing left to train, you see
"Nothing left to train". If your browser blocks clipboard access, you see "Your browser didn't allow copying".

## Making your own plan

1. On the overview, click **New plan**.
2. Fill in **Name** (required), and optionally **Category** and **Description**.
3. Add skills in any of these ways:
   - **Paste from EVE**: in the game, copy a skill plan or your skill queue, click **Paste from EVE**, paste the text
     and click **Add skills**. Each line needs one skill and level, like `Gunnery 4` or `Gunnery IV`. Lines that
     could not be read are listed. Then click **Add the N skills found**.
   - **Add a skill**: pick a level (I to V), type a skill name and click the skill.
   - **Add everything a ship or module needs**: type a ship, module or drone name (at least two letters) and click it.
4. Missing prerequisites are added for you and kept ahead of the skills that need them.
5. Use **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove** on each line to change the order. Removing a level also removes the
   levels above it.
6. Click **Save plan**.

## Changing, copying and deleting plans

- **Edit** and **Delete** show on your own plans. Shared plans can only be changed by skill plan managers.
- **Copy to my plans** makes a personal copy of any plan you can see, named "<plan> (copy)", and opens it for editing.
  Use this to adjust a shared plan for yourself.
- Deleting a personal plan cannot be undone.

## Notifications

Skill Plans sends no notifications.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**A character shows "Skills not synced yet".** Its skills come from the character sheet. Wait for the next sync, or
check that the character's login still works.

**A character is missing from the plan page.** Only characters on your account are shown. If you have no characters,
the page says "Add a character to see your progress on this plan."

**The time left looks wrong.** It is an estimate from your current attributes and implants. It changes if you remap,
plug in different implants or use boosters.

**I can't see the Shared switch in the editor.** Only skill plan managers can share plans.

**A plan I had open now says "No such skill plan".** It was deleted, or it is someone else's personal plan.

**A paste added nothing.** You see "No skills found in that text" when no line is a skill and level. Use one skill per
line, like `Gunnery 4`.
