# Industry Projects — User guide

Industry Projects is your corporation's build board. Officers post projects such as "20 Hurricanes for the
deployment", split into the items they need. You claim part of an item, build it, deliver it in game and mark it
delivered. A manager checks it and confirms it. Confirmed deliveries can earn Corp Store points.

## Where to find it

- **Projects** in the sidebar: the board, with **Open** and **Finished** projects.
- **My contributions** in the sidebar: your claims and deliveries across every project.
- The **Industry projects** widget on the dashboard: up to four open projects with their progress.

You only see projects meant for you. A project may be for every member, or only for some states or groups. You get a
notification when a new project for you starts.

## Read a project

Each project card shows where to deliver, the deadline ("due ..."), and progress: "N of M items done" with the
percentage confirmed and the percentage claimed. **You're building** means you have an open claim on it. Progress
counts every item the same, whatever its quantity.

Open a project to see:

- **Deliver to** and **Due** (EVE time), and **For** (who the project is for).
- **What it needs**: one row per item, with "X of Y delivered · Z being built · N free", and "pts/unit" or "/unit"
  when the item pays Corp Store points or ISK.
- Who is building each item, and their status: **Building**, **Delivered** (waiting for a manager) or
  **Confirmed**.
- **Contributors**: everyone who claimed something, with units delivered, units in progress and points earned.

## Claim an item

1. Open the project.
2. Next to the item, click **Claim**. The button only shows while the project is open, the item has units free and
   you have no open claim on it.
3. Enter **How many**. You can't claim more than is free.
4. Click **Claim**.

You can have one open claim per item. Once a manager confirms it, you can claim more of that item.

## Change or release a claim

On your claim line (shown as **You**):

- **Change**: set a new quantity, then click **Change claim**.
- **Release**: give the units back so others can claim them.

You can only change or release a claim before you mark it delivered.

## Deliver

1. Build the items and put them where the project says (**Deliver to**).
2. On your claim line, click **Delivered**.
3. Optional: in **Where is it?** say where it is, for example "Corp hangar, 2nd division".
4. Click **Mark delivered**.

Managers are told. A manager then either confirms it or sends it back.

- **Confirmed**: you get a notification "Delivery confirmed", with the points it earned if any.
- **Sent back**: you get a notification with the reason. The claim is back to **Building**, with "Sent back: ..."
  next to it. Fix the problem and mark it delivered again.

## Industry job hint

Next to an open claim you may see a blue line such as "3 runs in production, ends in 2d 4h", "2 ready to deliver" or
"1 delivered from the job". It comes from your manufacturing and reaction jobs for that item started after you
claimed it: your own jobs and corporation jobs you installed. Only you and the managers see it. It is a hint only:
nothing is confirmed without a manager, and runs are not always units (ammunition makes many per run).

The hint needs your characters' industry jobs on the site. If you never see it, check that your characters'
logins include the industry job scopes.

## My contributions

**My contributions** shows **Being built**, **Delivered**, **Projects helped** and **Points earned**, and a table of
every claim with **Item**, **Project**, **Quantity**, **Status**, **Points** and **Claimed**. To mark something
delivered, open the project from the table.

## Points and ISK

- **Points per unit** are paid by the Corp Store plugin when a manager confirms your delivery. One delivery pays at
  most 1,000,000 points. If the Corp Store isn't switched on, the project says so and no points are paid.
- **ISK per unit** is only shown. Paying it is up to your corporation.

## Notifications

All under the category **Industry projects** (turn it off under **Settings → Notifications**):

| When | Notification |
| --- | --- |
| A project for you starts | "New industry project: ..." |
| A manager confirms your delivery | "Delivery confirmed: ..." |
| A manager sends your delivery back | "Delivery sent back: ...", with the reason |
| A project you delivered to is done | "... is done" |

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**There's no Claim button.** The project is closed, the item has nothing free, or you already have an open claim on
it (use **Change** instead).

**"Only N left to claim" / "Everything is claimed already".** Others claimed the rest. Claim less, or wait for
someone to release theirs.

**I can't change or release my claim.** You marked it delivered. Ask a manager to send it back first.

**My delivery hasn't been confirmed.** A manager has to check it in game. Managers can't confirm their own
deliveries, so another manager must do it. Deliveries on a closed project can't be confirmed until it is reopened.

**I see "No such project".** It is for other states or groups, or it was deleted.

**I didn't get points.** Check that the item shows "pts/unit" and that the Corp Store plugin is on.
