# Announcements — User guide

Announcements is where leadership posts news and orders: fleet schedules, rule changes, tax changes and anything
else members should know. You read them on their own page, on your dashboard and in the Bulletin on the landing
page. You only ever see announcements meant for you; some are for everyone, others only for certain states or
groups.

## Where to find it

- **Announcements** in the main menu opens the page (`/p/announcements`).
- The **Announcements** dashboard widget shows the latest three, with a "new" badge if you have unread ones.
- The **Bulletin** on the landing page (`/home`) shows the pinned or newest announcement in full, with up to three
  more beside it. Your administrators can switch the Bulletin off, so it may not appear on your site.
- Ctrl+K search finds announcements by words in the title or text.

## Reading announcements

1. Open **Announcements** from the menu.
2. Pinned announcements are at the top, marked **Pinned**. Below them come the rest, newest first.
3. Each announcement shows who posted it and when. "edited" after the date means it was changed after posting;
   hover over it to see when.
4. The coloured stripe on the left and the label show how important it is:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| (no label, blue stripe) | News |
| **Important** | Something you should read soon |
| **Urgent** | Something you must read now |

Unread announcements carry a **New** badge. Opening the page marks everything on it as read after a moment. The
**New** badges stay on screen until you leave, so you can still see what was new on this visit.

Links in an announcement only work if they point to a page on this site or an `https://` address.

### From the dashboard or the Bulletin

- Click an announcement in the widget or the Bulletin to jump to it on the Announcements page.
- In the Bulletin, long announcements are cut off with a fade. Click **Read the full announcement** to see the rest.
- **All announcements** (or "N more announcements") in the Bulletin header opens the full page.

## Notifications

When an announcement for you goes out, you get a notification under the bell in the top bar (and at
`/notifications`). Clicking it opens the Announcements page.

- Scheduled announcements notify you when they go out, not when they were written.
- Editing an announcement does not send the notification again.
- The writer can choose not to notify anyone for a given announcement.

You can mute announcement notifications:

1. Open **Settings** (`/settings`).
2. Under **Notifications**, switch off **Announcements**.

**Urgent** announcements still reach you when you have muted announcements.

Your corporation may also post announcements to a Discord channel. That is set up by your administrators, not in your
own settings.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**I can't see an announcement a friend can see.**
It is probably meant only for some states or groups that you are not in. Ask leadership if you think you should be
included.

**An announcement disappeared.**
Announcements can be given an end time, after which they are taken down. Leadership can also delete them.
Notifications already sent stay in your notification list.

**I keep getting announcement notifications I don't want.**
Mute the **Announcements** category in **Settings** under **Notifications**. Urgent ones will still arrive.

**I don't see Announcements in the menu.**
The plugin is for members only. If your account is a guest (for example, your character is not in the corporation or
alliance), you can't open it. It may also be switched off on your site.

**The widget says "New" but the page shows nothing new.**
The widget refreshes every five minutes. Opening the Announcements page marks everything as read.
