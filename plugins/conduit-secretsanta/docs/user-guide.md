# Secret Santa — User guide

Secret Santa is a gift exchange. You sign up, a draw gives you one person to send a gift to, and someone else sends
one to you. You know who you give to; you never learn who gives to you, unless the organisers show every pair after
the event.

## Where to find it

- **Secret Santa** in the main menu opens the list of events (`/p/secretsanta`). It shows the events you may join and
  the ones you're in.
- The **Secret Santa** dashboard widget shows your current event and who you're giving to, or an event you can still
  join.
- Notifications under the bell tell you when the draw is out, when your gift is on its way and more. Clicking one opens
  the event.

## Signing up

1. Open the event from the list.
2. Under **Sign up**, pick the character that should get your gift in **Send my gift to**. Your main is picked
   unless you choose another.
3. Optionally write a **Wish list or a hint**: ships, skins, PLEX, or just what you fly.
4. Click **Join**.

You sign up once, as a person. All your characters count as one participant, so you never draw one of your own alts.

Until the event ends you can change the character and the wish list and click **Save**. Your Secret Santa always sees
the latest.

### Withdrawing

Click **Withdraw** under **Your sign-up**. This works until the draw. After the draw, ask an organiser to take you
out.

## Who you're giving to

When the organisers send the results, you get a notification "Secret Santa: you're giving to …". The event page then
shows **You're giving to** with:

- the character to send the gift to (and their main, if it's an alt);
- their wish list;
- the gift deadline.

Nobody else sees who you drew. Organisers can look at the pairs if they need to, and that is recorded.

### Marking your gift sent

1. Send the gift in the game: a contract, ISK, or however you like.
2. On the event page, optionally write a **Note for them**, such as "Item exchange contract in Jita". The note
   doesn't name you, but a contract or a transfer in the game does show who made it.
3. Click **I've sent it**.

Your giftee gets a notification that their gift is on its way, with your note. Click **Not sent after all** if you
marked it by mistake; this works until they mark it received.

## Your own gift

**Your gift** shows whether your Secret Santa has sent theirs, and their note. You get a notification when it's sent.

When it arrives, click **I got it**. You can write a **Thank-you note** first, or send one later with **Send
thank-you**. It reaches your Secret Santa without telling you who they are.

## Reminders

Two days before the gift deadline, you get a reminder if you haven't marked your gift sent.

## After the event

Organisers end the event once the gifts are done. They can then choose to show every pair. If they do, **Who gave to
whom** appears on the event page for everyone who took part.

## Notifications

| Notification | When |
|---|---|
| Secret Santa: *event name* | A new event you may join opened (if the organiser chose to tell people) |
| Secret Santa: you're giving to … | The results were sent |
| Secret Santa: your gift is on its way | Your Secret Santa marked your gift sent |
| Secret Santa: your giftee got your gift | Your giftee marked your gift received, with their thank-you |
| Secret Santa: a thank-you from your giftee | Your giftee wrote a thank-you after marking it received |
| Secret Santa: gift due … | Two days before the deadline, if you haven't marked your gift sent |
| Secret Santa: you're now giving to … | Someone left and you have a new giftee |
| Secret Santa: you were taken out of … | An organiser took you out |

You can mute them in **Settings** (`/settings`) under **Notifications** → **Secret Santa**. "You're now giving to"
still reaches you, because your giftee changed.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**I can't see an event a friend can see.**
It's probably only for some states or groups that you're not in. Ask an organiser.

**I can't join: "Sign-up has closed".**
Sign-up ends at the time on the event. Joining after the draw isn't possible.

**I picked the wrong character.**
Pick the right one in **Send my gift to** and click **Save**. If your Secret Santa already sent the gift, tell an
organiser.

**My Secret Santa sent a gift to my old character.**
If a character you picked is no longer linked to your account, gifts go to your main. Keep the picked character up to
date.

**Someone left and I got a new giftee after I'd sent my gift.**
Your gift to the person who left doesn't count any more. Send one to your new giftee.

**I don't see Secret Santa in the menu.**
The plugin is for members only. It may also be switched off on your site.
