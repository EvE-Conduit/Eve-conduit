# Corp Store — User guide

The Corp Store lets you earn points for what you do for the corp and spend them on items that officers hand over in
game. Points come in by themselves when you refer a recruit, fly in fleets, mentor a new member or deliver to an
industry project. Officers can also give points by hand. This guide explains how to earn, buy and follow your orders.

## Where to find it

The **Corp Store** menu has these pages for members:

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Corp Store** (`/p/store`) | Your points, the items for sale and the **Points earned** leaderboard. |
| **My points** (`/p/store/me`) | Your orders and every point you earned and spent. |

The **Corp Store** dashboard widget shows your store points and how many you earned in the last 30 days. Click it to
open the store.

## Earning points

Points are added to your balance automatically. Which ones are switched on, and how many points each gives, is up to
your officers.

| Source | When you get points |
| --- | --- |
| **Referral** | A recruit named you as their referrer and was accepted. |
| **Fleet** | A fleet you have a FAT in ends. You are paid once per fleet, however many characters you brought. |
| **Mentoring** | A new member you mentor graduates. |
| **Industry project** | You deliver to an industry project. Each project sets its own points per delivery. |
| **Awarded by hand** | An officer gave (or took) points. The reason is shown. |

Each thing is paid only once. Some FATs do not pay for fleets: FATs you added by hand for your own characters, and the
FC's (or fleet creator's) own FATs unless the in-game fleet tracking saw them.

Each source only works if the plugin behind it (Recruitment, Fleets, Mentoring, Industry Projects) is installed.

## Buying an item

1. Open **Corp Store**.
2. Find the item. Each card shows the price, the description, how many are left, and the limit per member if there is
   one.
3. Press **Buy**. If you cannot buy it, the button says why: **Sold out**, **Limit reached** or **Need N more**.
4. In **Buy <item>**, set **How many**. **Left afterwards** shows your balance after buying.
5. Press **Buy for N points**.

The points are taken at once. The officers get a notification. An officer hands the item over in game (for example
by contract or in a hangar) and marks the order as handed over. You get a notification when that happens.

## Following and cancelling orders

Open **My points**. The **Orders** list shows each order with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Not handed over yet. You can still cancel it. |
| **Handed over** | An officer handed it over. Their note, if any, is shown. |
| **Cancelled** | You or an officer cancelled it. The points came back. |

To cancel a waiting order:

1. Press **Cancel** next to the order.
2. Press **Cancel order** in **Cancel this order?**.

You get your points back, and the item goes back into stock.

## Your points history

The **Points** table on **My points** lists every entry, newest first: **What for**, **From** (the source),
**Points** and **When**. Purchases show as **Order** and refunds as **Refund**. The total at the top is your balance.

## The leaderboard

**Points earned** on the store page shows the top 10 earners. Switch between **This month** and **All time**. Only
points earned count. Spending does not lower your place, and refunds do not count.

## Notifications

All store notifications are in the **Corp Store** notification category.

| Notification | When |
| --- | --- |
| "You earned N store points" | Points were added to your balance. |
| "N store points were taken off" | An officer took points away. |
| "Your store order is ready: ..." | An officer marked your order as handed over. |
| "Your store order was cancelled: ..." | An officer cancelled your order. It includes their reason. |

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**The Buy button says "Need N more".**
You do not have enough points yet.

**The Buy button says "Limit reached".**
You already ordered the most of this item you are allowed. Cancelled orders do not count towards the limit.

**"Only N left" or "Sold out".**
There is not enough stock. Officers may add more later.

**I flew in a fleet but got no points.**
Fleet points may be switched off, or your FAT may not count (see Earning points). Points are paid when the fleet
ends, not while it runs.

**Can my balance go below zero?**
Yes, if an officer takes points away.

**I cannot cancel my order.**
Only waiting orders can be cancelled. Once it is handed over or cancelled, it cannot be changed.
