# Moon Mining Ledger — User guide

The Moon Mining Ledger shows what your characters mined from your corporation's moon drills each month, what that ore
is worth, and the moon tax you owe for it. When a month is closed you get a notification with the amount and how to
pay.

## Where to find it

- **Moon mining** in the sidebar opens **My moon mining**. (Officers with the full ledger land on the **Ledger**
  instead; their **Mine** button opens their own page.)
- The **Moon mining** widget on the dashboard shows what you mined this month, the estimated tax, and either the
  amount you owe or **All paid**. Click it to open **My moon mining**.

## My moon mining

1. Open **Moon mining** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a month in the month picker at the top right. The current month is marked "so far"; closed months are marked
   "closed".
3. Read the page:
   - **Mined in <month>**: the value of the ore your characters mined, and the number of units.
   - **Moon tax · N%**: the tax for that month. Under it: "estimate until the month is closed", "not paid yet",
     "paid" or "nothing owed".
   - **Characters**: how many of your characters mined that month.
   - **By character**: units and value for each of your characters.
   - **Moon tax history**: every month you were billed for, with the amount and **Paid** or **Unpaid**.

If you owe tax for any month, a yellow box at the top says "You owe ... in moon tax", with the payment
instructions your corporation set.

## How the value and tax are worked out

- Value = units mined x the ore's price. Prices are CCP's average market prices.
- While a month is open, values use today's prices and the tax is only an estimate.
- When officers close a month, the prices and the tax rate are fixed. Your bill for that month doesn't change after
  that, unless officers reopen the month.
- Tax = the month's tax rate (%) x the value of everything your characters mined, rounded to whole ISK.

Only characters registered on your account count toward your bill. Mining is grouped by account, so all your alts
are added up.

## Paying

1. Follow the payment instructions shown on **My moon mining** (or in the notification). If there are none, ask your
   corporation's leadership how to pay.
2. An officer marks your bill as paid once the ISK arrives. You can't mark it yourself.
3. Check **Moon tax history**: the month changes from **Unpaid** to **Paid**.

## Notifications

| When | Notification |
| --- | --- |
| A month you mined in is closed and you owe tax | "Moon tax for <month>: N ISK", with the payment instructions |

The category is **Moon tax**. You can turn it off under **Settings → Notifications**, but you still owe the tax.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**My mining doesn't show up.**
The data comes from the corporation's moon drill reports, which the site reads with an officer's login roughly every
hour. Check again later.

**I mined, but it says "You didn't mine from the moons in <month>".**
Check that the character that mined is added to your account on this site. Mining by characters nobody has registered
is not on your page. Also check the month: mining counts in the month the game reports it.

**My tax changed.**
The month is still open, so the value follows today's ore prices. It stops changing once the month is closed.

**I paid but it still says Unpaid.**
An officer has to mark it. Contact the person who handles moon tax.

**My bill disappeared.**
Officers reopened the month. You get a new bill when it is closed again.
