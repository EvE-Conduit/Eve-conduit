# Leaderboard — User guide

The Leaderboard ranks members by what their characters did: kills, ISK destroyed, fleets, mining, bounties, industry,
skillpoints and losses. All your characters count together for your main. At the end of each month the top three of
each board get a medal. You can hide yourself from the boards at any time.

## Where to find it

- **Leaderboard** in the site menu opens the overview, titled **Who's on top** (`/p/leaderboard`).
- The **Leaderboard** dashboard widget shows your place on each board this month. If you are hidden, it says
  "You're hidden from the boards."

## Who is on the boards

- Members (people in a members' state) with a main character.
- Only the corporations your admins picked, if they picked any.
- Not members who hid themselves.
- A member only shows on a board once they have a score above zero.

Figures come from the data the site already syncs for the character sheet. Only characters with a working login
count. New activity can take about an hour to show, and boards are cached for five minutes.

## The boards

Your admins choose which boards are shown.

| Board | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Kills** | Killmails your characters were on. Two of your characters on one kill count once. |
| **ISK destroyed** | Value of the ships you helped kill. |
| **Fleets** | Fleets flown in, from the Fleets plugin's FATs. Several characters in one fleet count once. FATs you added by hand for your own characters do not count. Only shown when the Fleets plugin is on. |
| **Mining** | Value of the ore and ice mined. |
| **Bounties** | NPC bounties and ESS payouts earned. |
| **Industry** | Industry jobs started. |
| **Skillpoints** | Total skillpoints across your characters. This board is always all time. |
| **Losses** | Ships lost. |

## Using the overview

1. Open **Leaderboard**.
2. Pick a period in the period list: this month (marked "so far"), one of the 12 months before it, or **All time**.
3. If members are in more than one corporation, pick a corporation, or **Every corporation**.

Each board card shows the top three and either "You're Nth of N" with your score, or how many members are on the
board. If you have medals, **Your medals** shows your gold, silver and bronze count.

## Opening a full board

1. On a board card, click **Full board**.
2. The page shows **On the board**, the total for everyone, and **Your place**.
3. The table lists the top places with **Member**, the score and, for most boards, a second figure (for example ISK
   destroyed next to kills).
4. If you are further down than the places shown, your own row appears at the bottom after "· · ·".
5. Click a row to see which of that member's characters earned the score. This works only if your admins left
   **Show characters** on. You can always see your own characters' share.

Press **All boards** to go back.

## Medals

1. Open **Leaderboard** and press **Medals**.
2. **Hall of fame** lists the members with the most medals, gold first.
3. Below it, each month lists the medals handed out: board, place, member and score.

Medals are handed out the day after a month ends, to the top three of each monthly board. Members who tie share the
place, so a tie for first gives two golds. The **Skillpoints** board does not give medals, because it is always all
time.

## Hiding yourself

1. On **Leaderboard**, press the eye button ("Hide me from the leaderboard").
2. You are taken off every board. A notice says "You're hidden from the leaderboard".
3. To come back, press **Show me** on that notice.

While hidden, you do not get medals, because you are not on the boards when they are handed out. Medals you already
have stay.

## Notifications

When you win a medal, you get "You finished 1st in <board> for <month>" (or 2nd, 3rd). It is in the
**Leaderboard medals** notification category, and links to the **Medals** page.

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**I am not on a board.**
Check that you have a main character, that you are not hidden, and that your main is in a corporation that competes.
You also need a score above zero for that period.

**My kills or mining are missing.**
The figures come from the character sheet. Check that the character's login still works and gives the site access to
that data. New activity can take about an hour to appear.

**A board I used to see is gone.**
Your admins switched it off. **Fleets** also disappears when the Fleets plugin is switched off.

**I cannot see which characters earned someone's score.**
Your admins turned off **Show characters**.

**My skillpoints are the same for every month.**
That is expected. The **Skillpoints** board always shows the total.
